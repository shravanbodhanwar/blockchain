❓ How This NFT Marketplace Works

🎨 Minting

An NFT is created by the smart contract and assigned a unique token ID. The metadata (name, image) is stored off-chain on IPFS; only the URI is recorded on-chain.

👤 Ownership

The NFT contract records which wallet owns each token via ownerOf(tokenId). This is the blockchain source of truth.

💰 Selling

The owner approves the marketplace contract, then lists the NFT at a price. The NFT stays in your wallet until someone buys it.

🛒 Buying

The buyer sends Sepolia ETH through the marketplace. The contract transfers the NFT to the buyer and ETH to the seller atomically.

📖 Reading (Free)

Reading data (ownership, metadata, listings) uses view functions — no gas, no MetaMask popup.

✍️ Writing (Gas)

Minting, listing, buying, and transferring create real transactions requiring Sepolia ETH. MetaMask signs — your private key never leaves your wallet.

User → Website → ethers.js → MetaMask → Ethereum Sepolia → [UserStorage | NFT Contract | Marketplace] → Blockchain
⚠️ Demo note: Ethereum Sepolia blockchain data is publicly readable. Do not store passwords, private keys, financial secrets, or sensitive personal information. NFT ownership and transaction history are publicly visible.
